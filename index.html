About Us

Precision, Innovation &
Uncompromising Quality

METAL BRIDGES is a professional welding and metal fabrication company dedicated to producing durable, innovative, and high-quality metal structures across Ghana.

Who We Are

Founded with a vision to revolutionize metal fabrication in Ghana, METAL BRIDGES has grown into one of the most trusted names in the industry. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to deliver exceptional results on every project.

Our state-of-the-art workshop in Tantra Hill, Accra, is equipped with the latest tools and machinery, enabling us to handle projects of any scale — from residential gates and security doors to large industrial structures and architectural masterpieces.

Kofi Boansi

Modern Metal Designer

With a deep passion for craftsmanship and innovation, Kofi Boansi leads METAL BRIDGES with an unwavering commitment to precision, quality, and customer satisfaction. His vision drives the company to continuously push the boundaries of what's possible in metal fabrication, transforming raw steel into functional works of art that stand the test of time.

Under his leadership, METAL BRIDGES has completed over 150 projects across residential, commercial, and industrial sectors, earning a reputation for reliability, excellence, and modern design sensibility.
